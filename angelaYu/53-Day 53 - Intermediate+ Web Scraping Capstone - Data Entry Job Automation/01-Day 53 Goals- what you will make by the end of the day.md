### 1. The Capstone: Automate a Data Entry Job

Data entry = moving data from one place to another. The dream Reddit post: people who
automated their own jobs. This capstone does exactly that:

> Scrape **real-estate listings** from a listings site → automatically fill and submit
> a **Google Form** with each listing's address, price and link.
