# What's the correct solution- What's the best answer- What's the right way

---

### 1. The Message

*"Is my solution the right one?"* — there is **no single right solution**. Code that:

* works correctly,
* is readable by another human,
* can be extended without pain,

…is a good solution, however different it looks from the walkthrough. What you can do is
compare approaches, borrow ideas, and progressively refine — exactly what code review in
a real team looks like.
