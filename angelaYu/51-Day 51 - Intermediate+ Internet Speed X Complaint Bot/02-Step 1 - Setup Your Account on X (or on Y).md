Here is a structured breakdown of this lesson on setting up the account.

---

### 1. Prerequisites

* An X (or practice-site) account with email + password.
* Log in manually once in the Chrome profile Selenium attaches to, so there are no
  captcha surprises on the day the bot runs.

---

### 2. A Note on Ethics

Publicly shaming a company is powerful — automate it *only* for truthful complaints
from your own account. The mechanics here (login, type, submit) are the reusable part.

---

### Summary Checklist

1. Account + one manual login = ready.
2. Truthful complaints only.
