# Why is this -so- Hard-! Can I really do this

---

### 1. The Message

Learning a brand-new skill as an adult feels *painful* — and that's normal. Angela
compares it to learning to windsurf: falling in the water repeatedly is not failure, it's
**the process of learning**.

* The struggle **is** the progress — the more you struggle, the more you're learning.
* Don't interpret difficulty as "this isn't for me". Everyone feels it.
