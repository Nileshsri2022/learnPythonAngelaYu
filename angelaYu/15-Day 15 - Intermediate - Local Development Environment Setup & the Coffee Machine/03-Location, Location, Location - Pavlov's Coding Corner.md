# Location, Location, Location - Pavlov's Coding Corner

---

### 1. The Message: Pavlov's Coding Corner

Study in the **same dedicated spot** every time — your own "coding corner". The brain
builds associations between location and focus (classical conditioning — Pavlov's dogs,
but for programmers):

* Same chair/desk/room → the brain switches into study mode on arrival.
* Avoid studying in bed or on the sofa — mixed signals weaken the habit.
