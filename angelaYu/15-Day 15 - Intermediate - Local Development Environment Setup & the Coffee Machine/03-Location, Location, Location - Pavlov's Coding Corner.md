### Summary

Well done for completing day 15. You&#x27;re doing really, really well. Now, today, I got another study tip for you and the study tip relates to where you study. Because what I find is that when I mix the area where I have all my entertainment, where I watch Netflix, where I eat or where I have fun with the place where I study, then I can start to get a bit confused and my brain is like, &quot;Hey, wait a minute. We used to watch Netflix here. Now why are we doing this really hard thing? I just want to go back to that Netflix watching and maybe some popcorn.&quot; So what you can do is define a study area, find a little nook and cranny, go under the stairs if you have to. Just make sure that you only study in that one place and you don&#x27;t do anything else. That way every time you enter that space, it&#x27;ll feel like, &quot;Oh, I know. I know what I have to do here. I have to study.&quot; And this is one tip that&#x27;s really helped me beat procrastination. So I hope you&#x27;ll try it out too.
