### 2. The End-of-Day Project: Band Name Generator

A small program your friend can run to name their new band. It works like this:

1. Asks **what city you grew up in** (e.g. `Bristol`)
2. Asks **the name of your pet** (e.g. `Rabbit`)
3. Prints the two combined — the band name: **`Bristol Rabbit`**

> **Tip:** Try the finished program via the preview link in the course resources
> *before* building it, so you know exactly what you're aiming for.
