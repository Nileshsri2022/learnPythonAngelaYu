# Choose Your Destiny!

---

### 1. The Idea

The project ships with TSLA as the example — but the *skills* transfer to any ticker and
any alert. The course invites you to adapt it:

* Track a different stock (or crypto via another API).
* Change the threshold (±5% → ±1% for more alerts).
* Change the delivery (email instead of SMS).

Everything you've built since Day 32 is composable — swapping data sources and outputs
is now a few lines each.
