# Space out your study sessions and stay consistent

---

### 1. The Message: Space It Out

Cramming fades; **spaced repetition** sticks. Short, consistent sessions beat marathon
binges:

* 30–60 focused minutes daily > 6 hours once a week.
* Sleep between sessions consolidates what you learned.
* Revisit old projects occasionally — anything you can rebuild from memory is truly yours.
