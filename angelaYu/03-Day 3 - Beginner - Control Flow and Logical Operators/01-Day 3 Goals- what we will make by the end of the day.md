### 1. Skills Covered on Day 3

* **Conditional statements** — `if` / `else` / `elif`
* **Comparison operators** — `>`, `<`, `>=`, `<=`, `==`, `!=`
* The **modulo operator** `%`
* **Nested `if` statements** and **multiple `if` statements in succession**
* **Logical operators** — `and`, `or`, `not`
* Code blocks, indentation and scope

---

### 2. The End-of-Day Project: Treasure Island

A **choose-your-own-adventure** game where the story branches on the user's choices:

1. *"You're at a crossroad… Type 'left' or 'right'."*
2. Left → a lake: *"Type 'wait' to wait for a boat. Type 'swim' to swim across."*
3. Wait → a house with **three doors** — red, yellow and blue. Pick the right one to win; the wrong one… **Game Over**.

> **Tip:** The fun of this project is writing **your own story** — the conditionals
> are just the machinery that makes it branch.
