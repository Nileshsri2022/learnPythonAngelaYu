Here is a structured breakdown of this lesson on the goals for Day 52.

---

### 1. The Follower Bot

The classic growth-hack: log into Instagram (or the course's **Share-a-Naan** naan-bread
clone), visit a big target account, open its **followers list**, and follow them all —
a chunk of them follow back.

---

### 2. The Flow

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1. Credentials | env vars, never literals |
| 2. Class | `InstagramBot` — state + behaviour |
| 3. Login | form fill, Day 48 verbs |
| 4. Followers | open the modal, scroll it to load everyone |
| 5. Follow | loop over usernames, click Follow |

---

### Summary Checklist

1. Login → scrape the follower list → follow loop.
2. Scrolling a modal is today's new Selenium trick.
