# The Benefits of Daily Practice

---

### 1. The Message

Keep showing up — but don't panic if one "day" takes two or three real days. Angela built
this course while working as a doctor, studying after night shifts. The rule is **daily
repetition, not daily perfection**:

* Consistency keeps the content fresh in your mind.
* Long breaks are the enemy; slow progress is still progress.
* Follow the order — it's a linear path, and you'll get there.

---

### Summary Checklist

1. A "day" of the course = a *unit of work*, not a deadline.
2. Short, steady practice beats occasional heroic sessions.
