### Summary

Hey, guys. Welcome to Day 7 of 100 Days of Code. Now, today we have a whole day dedicated to a really cool project. And what we&#x27;re going to be building throughout this day is basically Hangman or the code equivalent of this game. So the way the game works is you have to guess a word, and for every wrong letter you submit, you end up taking a life away from this little man. And of course, the longer it takes you to get to the word, the more you put your little man in danger. So this is what the final completed project is going to look like. There is a random word that I have to guess, and I&#x27;m going to type a letter to see if it&#x27;s in that word, if it&#x27;s not, my hangman starts, and the little character starts being drawn. But if I do get a letter right, then that process pauses and the letter gets added to the word that I have to guess. And the goal is to be able to guess all the right letters so that eventually you figure out what the right word is. And this is an extremely, extremely difficult game. It took me many tries to get a take where I actually managed to guess the word before the hangman died. But it is quite fun and you can check it out by simply going to this URL at the top or going over there through the Course Resources where there is a link for you as well. But this is what you&#x27;re going to be building by the end of today. But just by building this game, we&#x27;re going to be able to review and test ourselves on a bunch of concepts that we&#x27;ve already covered, like for and while Loops, or if and else Statements, Lists, Strings, the range() function, Modules, and much, much more. So I hope you&#x27;re as excited as I am to get started on this project. And once you&#x27;re ready, head over to the next lesson and let&#x27;s begin!
