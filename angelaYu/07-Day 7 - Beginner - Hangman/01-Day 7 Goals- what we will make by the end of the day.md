# Day 7 Goals- what we will make by the end of the day

---

### 1. The Project: Hangman

A full day dedicated to one project — the classic game of Hangman:

1. A random word is chosen secretly.
2. You guess one letter at a time.
3. Correct letters fill into the blanks; wrong guesses draw the hangman.
4. Guess the whole word before the drawing completes — or lose.

---

### 2. Why a Whole Day on One Project?

Hangman quietly **reviews everything so far**:

* `for` and `while` loops
* `if` / `else` statements
* Lists, strings and the `range()` function
* Modules (`random`, plus importing your own files)

It also teaches the **process**: breaking a complex problem into steps with a flowchart,
then building it piece by piece.

---

### Summary Checklist

1. Day 7 = project day: one game, five incremental steps.
2. Every previous concept gets exercised in real combination.
3. Learn the workflow: flowchart → steps 1–5 → finished game.
