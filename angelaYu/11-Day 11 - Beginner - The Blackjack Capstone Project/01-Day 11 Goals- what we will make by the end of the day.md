### 1. What is a Capstone Project?

A **capstone** stretches you by combining *everything* learned so far into one bigger build.
No new theory today — just application:

* `while` and `for` loops
* Functions with inputs and outputs (`return`)
* Lists, dictionaries and the `random` module
* `if`/`elif`/`else` logic and edge cases

---

### 2. The Project: Blackjack (21)

The classic casino card game, played against the computer:

1. Both player and dealer are dealt two cards.
2. Cards add up — get as close to **21** as possible **without going over**.
3. Player keeps drawing or stands; the dealer must draw until reaching 17.
4. Best score wins; over 21 = instant bust.

The build is guided by **14 progressive hints** — the course's structure for big projects.
