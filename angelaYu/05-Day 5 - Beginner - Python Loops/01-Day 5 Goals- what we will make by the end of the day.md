### 2. The End-of-Day Project: PyPassword Generator

Companies keep getting hacked, and the #1 cause is **password reuse** (check your own email
at haveibeenpwned.com!). The fix: long, unique, random passwords — which your program will
generate:

1. *How many letters?* → e.g. `14`
2. *How many symbols?* → e.g. `3`
3. *How many numbers?* → e.g. `4`
4. Prints an impossible-to-crack password mixing all three.

**Two difficulty levels:**

* **Easy** — password in sequence: all letters, then symbols, then numbers.
* **Hard** — characters in a **completely random order**.
