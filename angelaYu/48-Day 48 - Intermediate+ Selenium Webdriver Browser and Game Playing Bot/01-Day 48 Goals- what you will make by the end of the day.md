Here is a structured breakdown of this lesson on the goals for Day 48.

---

### 1. Selenium: Driving a Real Browser

BeautifulSoup reads HTML that *you* download. **Selenium WebDriver** opens a real
Chrome window and drives it — clicking, typing, scrolling — exactly like a human user.
That unlocks pages BeautifulSoup can't touch: JavaScript-rendered content, logins,
interactive games.

---

### 2. Today's Arc

1. Install and set up Selenium.
2. Find and select elements.
3. Fill forms, click buttons.
4. Project: a bot that plays **Cookie Clicker** for you.

---

### Summary Checklist

1. Selenium = browser automation, not just scraping.
2. JS-rendered pages and interactions are now in range.
