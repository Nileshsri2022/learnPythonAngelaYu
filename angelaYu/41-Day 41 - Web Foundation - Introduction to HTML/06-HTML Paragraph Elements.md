Here is a structured breakdown of this lesson on paragraph elements.

---

### 1. The Paragraph Element

```html
<p>
    This is a paragraph. Browsers automatically add space above and
    below paragraphs — that spacing comes from the element's default style.
</p>
```

* `<p>` marks a block of body text.
* Multiple lines inside the source collapse into flowing text — line breaks must be
  marked up (next lesson's `<br>`).

---

### 2. Headings + Paragraphs = Documents

```html
<h1>My Favourite Foods</h1>
<p>Nothing beats a plate of galouti kebabs from Lucknow.</p>
<h2>Why kebabs win</h2>
<p>They melt before you chew. That's the whole argument.</p>
```

Headings label sections; paragraphs fill them — a complete, accessible page with just
two elements.

---

### Summary Checklist

1. `<p>` for prose blocks.
2. White space in the source ≠ layout on the page.
