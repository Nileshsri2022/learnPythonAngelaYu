# Don't forget to review occasionally

---

### 1. The Message: Review Occasionally

Sixteen days is a lot of material — and it fades without review. Schedule quick revisits:

* Re-do an old exercise **without looking** (the calendar-reminder trick from Day 14).
* Skim your own notes; rewrite anything that doesn't click.
* A 20-minute review today saves hours of re-learning later.
