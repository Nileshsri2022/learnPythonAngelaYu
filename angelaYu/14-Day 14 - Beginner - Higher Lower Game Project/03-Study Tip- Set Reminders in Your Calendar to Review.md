# Study Tip- Set Reminders in Your Calendar to Review

---

### 1. The Message: Calendar Your Reviews

When you needed the solution to get unstuck, that concept isn't truly yours yet. **Set a
calendar reminder** a week or so out to redo that exercise from scratch:

* Looking at a solution ≠ being able to reproduce it.
* Spaced review converts "I saw the answer" into "I can write it".
* This is the Beginner track complete — 14 days, real momentum.
