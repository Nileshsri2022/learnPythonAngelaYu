### Summary

Well done on completing day 14. Now, I have a study tip for you today, and that study tip is all about putting things in the calendar. So let&#x27;s say that you complete one of the days and in one of the exercises, you got stuck and you had to look at the solution. Don&#x27;t worry, that doesn&#x27;t mean you failed. That just means that you&#x27;re learning. But the important thing is when you look at the solution, that you actually really make sure you understand it. So not just read the code, but maybe Google some things around it, maybe rewatch some lectures, make sure that it makes sense and that you understand what&#x27;s happening. Then go into your calendar and schedule to come back in a week and redo that exercise or code challenge. At that point, hopefully, you&#x27;ve already embodied and absorbed all the knowledge and you understand what happened last time so you can complete it in the first go. So that&#x27;s my tip for you. Put things in the calendar to review if you get stuck, and that&#x27;s going to help you overcome more hurdles.
