### 1. Prepare Once, Automate Forever

Create the account (Tinder, or the course's Tindog clone so nobody's real love life is
automated) and log in manually at least once in the Chrome profile Selenium uses.

* Manual first login solves phone-number/OTP steps the bot can't see.
* The site's markup changes often — pin your selectors from DevTools on the day you run.
