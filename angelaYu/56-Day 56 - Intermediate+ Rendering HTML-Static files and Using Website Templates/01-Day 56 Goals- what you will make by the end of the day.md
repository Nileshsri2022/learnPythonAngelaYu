Here is a structured breakdown of this lesson on the goals for Day 56.

---

### 1. From Strings to Files

Returning HTML strings doesn't scale. Today Flask learns to serve:

1. **HTML files** — real pages from a `templates/` folder.
2. **Static files** — CSS, JavaScript and images from a `static/` folder.
3. **Pre-made templates** — professional designs you drop your content into.

---

### 2. The Project

A **name card website**: pick a free HTML template, customise it with your details,
serve it with Flask.

---

### Summary Checklist

1. `templates/` for pages, `static/` for assets.
2. Real developers start from templates, not blank files.
