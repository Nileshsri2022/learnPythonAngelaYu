# History of GUI and Introduction to Tkinter

---

### 1. What is a GUI?

Until now every program was console-based. A **Graphical User Interface** — windows,
buttons, text fields — is how ordinary software looks. **Tkinter** is Python's built-in
binding to Tk, one of the oldest GUI toolkits.

---

### 2. Why Tkinter Matters Here

* **Zero install** — ships with Python, like `turtle` and `random`.
* The concepts (widgets, layout, event callbacks) transfer to every other GUI/web
  framework you'll ever meet.
* Days 27–31 build four apps with it: converter, Pomodoro timer, password manager,
  flash cards.

---

### Summary Checklist

1. Tkinter = built-in, simple, good enough for real desktop tools.
2. Widgets are objects; you configure and place them.
