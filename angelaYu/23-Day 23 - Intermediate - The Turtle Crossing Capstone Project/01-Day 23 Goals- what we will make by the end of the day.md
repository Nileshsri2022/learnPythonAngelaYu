### 1. The Game (Crossy Road style)

A turtle tries to cross an endless road while cars stream past:

* `Up` key moves the turtle one step toward the top.
* Cars spawn at random heights on the right and drive left.
* Hit a car → GAME OVER.
* Reach the top → next level: **cars speed up** and the turtle resets.
* Difficulty selector (easy/medium/hard) sets the starting car speed.
