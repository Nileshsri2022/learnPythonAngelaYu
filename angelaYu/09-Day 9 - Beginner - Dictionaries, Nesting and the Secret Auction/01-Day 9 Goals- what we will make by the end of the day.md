### 2. The End-of-Day Project: Secret (Blind) Auction

In a silent auction nobody sees anyone else's bid:

1. A bidder types their **name** and **bid amount**.
2. The program clears the screen and asks if there are more bidders.
3. When bidding ends, the **highest bid wins** and is announced.

The bids are stored in a dictionary (name → amount) as the program loops — dictionaries
in action.

---

### Summary Checklist

1. Dictionaries **tag** related pieces of information together.
2. Nesting combines lists + dictionaries into rich structures.
3. The blind auction is one dictionary + one `while` loop + one `max()` lookup.
