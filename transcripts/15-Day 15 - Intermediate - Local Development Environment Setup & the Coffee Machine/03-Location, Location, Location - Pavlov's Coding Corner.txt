Well done for completing day 15. You're doing really, really well. Now, today, I got another study tip for you and the study tip relates to where you study. Because what I find is that when I mix the area where I have all my entertainment, where I watch Netflix, where I eat or where I have fun with the place where I study, then I can start to get a bit confused and my brain is like, "Hey, wait a minute. We used to watch Netflix here. Now why are we doing this really hard thing? I just want to go back to that Netflix watching and maybe some popcorn." So what you can do is define a study area, find a little nook and cranny, go under the stairs if you have to. Just make sure that you only study in that one place and you don't do anything else. That way every time you enter that space, it'll feel like, "Oh, I know. I know what I have to do here. I have to study." And this is one tip that's really helped me beat procrastination. So I hope you'll try it out too.
