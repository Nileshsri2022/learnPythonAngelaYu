So now that we understand all three components, what does it mean to make our API RESTful? Well, REST is essentially just an architectural style. So just as you have different buildings that have different architectural styles, for example, the Baroque architectural style with the nice arches or other buildings might be built in the Gothic architectural style with flying buttresses and these imposing beams. And REST actually came about really quite recently as a part of this guy's Ph.D. This is Roy Fielding and he did his PhD in the University of California. And as a part of his doctorate, he researched and came up with this architectural style that we now call REST.
